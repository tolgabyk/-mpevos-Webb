---
title: A compulsory internship program for university students
description: IMPEVOS is accepting applications from university students who need to complete a compulsory internship with a product team.
date: "2026-06-01"
---

IMPEVOS opened an internship program for university students who have a compulsory internship. The application asks for a name, university, department, and CV.

The program is meant for students who will spend that internship inside product work. Capacity and term details are confirmed after you apply.

The form is in the Careers section on the homepage. Questions can go to kariyer@impevos.com.tr.
